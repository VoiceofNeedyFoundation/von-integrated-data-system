## Review limitations

- Gemini meeting summaries can contain transcription errors; business-critical statements require analyst or VoN confirmation.
- Drive shortcuts were inventoried but their target files were not automatically treated as reviewed.
- Every spreadsheet in the supplied folder tree was structurally opened, but detailed cell-level profiling is currently concentrated on the master and primary consolidation sources.
- This register records evidence, not authority. The source-authority hierarchy is still pending approval.
